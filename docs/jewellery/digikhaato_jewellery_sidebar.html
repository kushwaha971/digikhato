Billing & Sales
Create, print, share, and manage all sales-side documents. Supports GST and non-GST flows, old gold exchange, e-invoice, and multi-mode payments.
Tax invoice GST
  • B2C and B2B invoice types
  • CGST / SGST / IGST split
  • IRN + QR via GSP
  • E-way bill data prep
Estimate / Quotation
  • No tax impact
  • One-click convert to invoice
  • PDF share via WhatsApp
Old gold exchange
  • Purity test entry
  • URD purchase voucher
  • Deduction from bill total
Payments
  • Cash + UPI + Card + Bank
  • Advance adjustment
  • UPI deeplink on invoice
Print & share
  • A4, A5, thermal 80/58mm
  • Multi-template per tenant
  • WhatsApp / SMS / Email
Sale return Credit note
  • Linked to original invoice
  • GST credit note generated
  • Refund mode selection
